How to take a soil sample for a DNA test

You take the sample yourself, with no kit to wait for: a sterile container from any pharmacy and a clean spade. These are the seven steps we send with every BioFiling Next order.

The seven steps

  1. What you need: new disposable gloves, a marker, a clean spade or spatula (disposable or stainless steel) and a sterile urine container from any pharmacy, or a sterile bag. Clean the tool with ethanol and let it dry before and between samples.
  2. Where: a spot that represents the field, at least 15 m from roads, forest belts and field edges. Avoid hollows, places where puddles form, and spots with manure, fertiliser, fire or spilled fuel or agrochemicals, unless you want exactly those tested.
  3. How deep and how much: soil from 3 to 10 cm, together with the plant residues in it, about 5 ml per point.
  4. How many points: 3 to 12 points in the same block go into one container or bag: that is one sample for that field. As a guide: 1 ha 5 points, 2–5 ha 7, 6–10 ha 10, 11–50 ha 15, 51–100 ha 20.
  5. Label it: close the container tightly, write for example “sample 1, field 1” on it, and save the GPS coordinates of the points on your phone.
  6. Next field: change gloves and clean the tool. Sample unfrozen soil (above 0 °C), or 3 weeks after thawing.
  7. Send it: put the container in a box with a note of the sample numbers and coordinates, and send it one way to the nearest sample collection point; its address comes with your order confirmation.

One sample per field

Each container is one sample: the soil of one field or block, mixed from 3 to 12 points. If two parts of a field differ, for example a good part and a problem part, take one sample from each and compare the two reports.

When to sample

Sample unfrozen soil, above 0 °C, or 3 weeks after thawing. The point is to read the soil before you choose the crop, the variety and the protection plan, so sample before sowing or planting.

After you send it

Your report usually comes 3 to 4 weeks from the day we receive your sample, at most 6, depending on how complex the DNA extraction from your sample is and on current workload. It names every pathogen found with its quantity, the crops it threatens, the beneficial organisms that suppress it, and a written conclusion.

Price and how to order
