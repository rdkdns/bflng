What we collect and why

  • Sample report sign-up: your email address and your consent tick. We use them to open the full sample report for you and to write to you about BioFiling. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Requests by email or through the request form: your email address and what you choose to tell us, such as the number of samples, country, crops and your question. We use them to answer you and, if you ask, to prepare an offer. Legal basis: steps before a contract at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Orders and payment: on Stripe's payment page you give your name, email, billing address, phone and, if you wish, your business name, VAT number and crops, and you enter your payment details there. We receive the order details and the payment status, never your full card number. We use them to carry out your order, invoice it and keep the accounting records the law requires. Legal basis: the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Giving your email address is needed for us to answer you or send a report. This site loads nothing from advertising or social networks. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, our own step counter and Google Analytics 4 (with IP anonymisation) to measure site traffic and how parts of the page are used. We make no automated decisions about you.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this site.

Cloudflare, Google and Stripe may process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Switzerland, where Migadu is based, is recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate protection.

How long we keep it

  • Requests that do not lead to an order: up to 2 years after our last contact.
  • Sample report sign-ups: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Orders and invoices: 7 years, as the Estonian Accounting Act requires.

Your rights
