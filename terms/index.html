3. What we sell

BioFiling standard soil DNA analysis: a laboratory DNA analysis of each soil sample you send, covering fungal pathogens and oomycetes, bacterial pathogens, a biodiversity index, the fungi-to-bacteria balance and pathogenicity for the crops you name. You receive a written report with an interpretation for those crops.

BioFiling assesses microbiological signals. Detecting an organism in soil does not by itself establish active plant disease. The analysis does not measure nutrients or other chemical and physical properties of the soil.

5. How an order works

  1. You choose the price tier and the number of samples on this site and pay online. The contract is made when your payment is received. For volume orders, it is made when you accept our written offer.
  2. We send you the sampling instructions and the laboratory address.
  3. You take the samples as instructed, in a sterile container (a urine container from any pharmacy is suitable) or a sterile bag, and send them one way to the laboratory. No sampling kit is needed.
  4. The samples are analysed and interpreted, and the report is sent to you as a PDF by email.

Results in the same time as the BeCrop Test or sooner are possible (Biome Makers states usually 4 to 6 weeks from the arrival of samples at its lab). We confirm the expected date for each order. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline.
