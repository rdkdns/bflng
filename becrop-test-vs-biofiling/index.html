BeCrop Test or BioFiling: two soil DNA tests, two different reports

Both tests sequence the DNA in a soil sample. They answer different questions. The BeCrop Test from Biome Makers turns the data into indices of soil function. BioFiling lists the organisms found, pathogens and their antagonists, species by species with quantities, and interprets them for the crop you name.

Which one fits your question

The BeCrop Test fits when

  • you want indices of soil function, such as nutrient cycling and stress adaptation;
  • you want an online portal with maps and comparisons across fields and seasons;
  • you want to order up to ten tests online at a fixed price.

BioFiling fits when

  • your question is which pathogens are in this field, by name, before a crop goes in;
  • you want to see which antagonists the soil already holds before you spend on biological protection;
  • you want a written interpretation for your crops, and to read a full real report before you order;
  • you want to start with one sample.

What the two tests share

  • Both read DNA. DNA shows that an organism is present, not that it is active. Soil also holds DNA from cells that are no longer alive, and a standard DNA result includes it.
  • Neither measures nutrients, organic matter or other soil chemistry. Both complement a chemical soil analysis.
  • Neither replaces walking the field. A result is a signal to weigh together with the crop, the weather and what you see.

A practical way to decide
