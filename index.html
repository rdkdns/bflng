The problem with guessing

The biggest risk to the crop may already be in the soil.

A field can look healthy and still carry the organisms that will cost you yield. Without biological data, crop and protection decisions rest on last year's symptoms.

01

Pathogens you cannot see

Soil-borne threats go unnoticed until plants show stress or disease, when the cheap options are gone.

02

Treatment aimed at the wrong cause

Choosing inputs without knowing which organisms are present can mean paying to treat a problem you do not have.

03

Losses nobody can explain

Repeated underperformance on the same field often has a biological cause that a chemistry test does not show.

From DNA data to a decision

Not a list of microbes. An answer about your crop.

Questions

Before you order.

Want to discuss crops, sampling or shipping first? Email our team ↗

Can I order a single sample?

The standard minimum order is 10 soil samples at €189 each, €1,890 in total before tax and shipping. If you need fewer, write to us and we will tell you what is possible.

Is BioFiling the same as an NPK soil test?

No. BioFiling analyses the soil microbiome and potential plant pathogens using DNA. It does not measure nutrients or other chemical and physical properties, and it complements those tests.

Does a detected pathogen mean my crop is diseased?

Not necessarily. Finding an organism in soil is a biological signal, not proof of active disease. What it means in practice depends on the crop's susceptibility, the amount present, the weather and other factors. The interpretation in the report addresses this.

How is BioFiling different from the BeCrop Test?

Both sequence soil DNA. According to Biome Makers' own pages (November 2026), the BeCrop Test costs €199 per test, can be bought singly, and delivers indices for nutrient cycling, disease risk and biodiversity in an online portal. BioFiling costs €189 per sample with a minimum of ten, concentrates on naming disease agents and their pathogenicity for your crop, and comes with a written interpretation. If you need one sample, BeCrop is easier to buy. If you need a disease-focused reading across several fields, that is what BioFiling is made for.

How is it different from PLFA tests such as Eurofins Soil Life Monitor?

They answer different questions. PLFA measures how much living microbial biomass there is and how it divides into broad groups. As Eurofins itself notes, it distinguishes groups, not species, so it cannot tell you which pathogen is present. BioFiling names the organisms but does not report biomass in mg per kg. Some farms use both.

Which organisms does the analysis cover?

Fungal pathogens and oomycetes, bacterial pathogens, a biodiversity index, the fungi-to-bacteria balance, and pathogenicity for specific crops. If you need a particular organism checked, ask us before ordering.

How soon will I get results?

It depends on shipping from your country and on the size of the order. We confirm the expected turnaround for your order instead of promising one figure for everyone.

Are VAT, shipping and sampling materials included?

The €189 price is before tax and shipping. Both depend on your country and are confirmed in writing, together with the sampling arrangements, before you pay.

Does the EU Soil Monitoring Law require farmers to run this test?

No. Directive (EU) 2025/2360 lists DNA metabarcoding of fungi and bacteria as the soil biodiversity descriptor, but it obliges Member States to monitor soils, not farmers to buy tests. BioFiling is a tool for your own crop decisions, not a compliance certificate.
