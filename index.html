How to take a sample: 7 steps
  1. What you need: new disposable gloves, a marker, a clean spade or spatula (disposable or stainless steel) and a sterile urine container from any pharmacy, or a sterile bag. Clean the tool with ethanol and let it dry before and between samples.
  2. Where: a spot that represents the field, at least 15 m from roads, forest belts and field edges. Avoid hollows, places where puddles form, and spots with manure, fertiliser, fire or spilled fuel or agrochemicals, unless you want exactly those tested.
  3. How deep and how much: soil from 3 to 10 cm, together with the plant residues in it, about 5 ml per point.
  4. How many points: 3 to 12 points in the same block go into one container or bag: that is one sample for that field. As a guide: 1 ha 5 points, 2–5 ha 7, 6–10 ha 10, 11–50 ha 15, 51–100 ha 20.
  5. Label it: close the container tightly, write for example “sample 1, field 1” on it, and save the GPS coordinates of the points on your phone.
  6. Next field: change gloves and clean the tool. Sample unfrozen soil (above 0 °C), or 3 weeks after thawing.
  7. Send it: put the container in a box with a note of the sample numbers and coordinates, and send it one way to the laboratory address we give you after your order.

Choose BioFiling when

  • a field keeps underperforming and chemistry does not explain it;
  • you want to know which disease agents are present before choosing a crop, variety or protection plan;
  • you want a written, disease-focused interpretation rather than a dashboard of indices;

Another test fits better when

  • you want the amount of living microbial biomass in mg per kg: PLFA measures quantity, DNA sequencing gives proportions;
  • you want a quick on-farm check of overall microbial activity: a respiration test does that;
  • you need nutrient levels: that is a chemistry test, and BioFiling does not replace it.

Can I order a single sample?

Yes. There is no minimum order. One to three samples cost €189 each, four or five cost €179 each, and six or more cost €169 each, VAT included where it applies, shipping extra. Lower prices can apply to larger batches under agreed conditions.

Is BioFiling the same as an NPK soil test?

No. BioFiling analyses the soil microbiome and potential plant pathogens using DNA. It does not measure nutrients or other chemical and physical properties, and it complements those tests.

Does a detected pathogen mean my crop is diseased?

Not necessarily. Finding an organism in soil is a biological signal, not proof of active disease. What it means in practice depends on the crop's susceptibility, the amount present, the weather and other factors. The interpretation in the report addresses this.

How is BioFiling different from the BeCrop Test?

Both sequence soil DNA. According to Biome Makers' own pages (November 2026), the BeCrop Test costs €199 per test and delivers indices for nutrient cycling, disease risk and biodiversity in an online portal. BioFiling costs €189 per sample for 1 to 3 samples, €179 for 4 or 5 and €169 for 6 or more, concentrates on naming disease agents and their pathogenicity for your crop, and comes with a written interpretation. If you want index scores in a portal, BeCrop provides that. If you need a disease-focused reading of your fields, that is what BioFiling is made for.

How is it different from PLFA tests such as Eurofins Soil Life Monitor?

They answer different questions. PLFA measures how much living microbial biomass there is and how it divides into broad groups. As Eurofins itself notes, it distinguishes groups, not species, so it cannot tell you which pathogen is present. BioFiling names the organisms but does not report biomass in mg per kg. Some farms use both.

Which organisms does the analysis cover?

Fungal pathogens and oomycetes, bacterial pathogens, a biodiversity index, the fungi-to-bacteria balance, and pathogenicity for specific crops. If you need a particular organism checked, ask us before ordering.

How soon will I get results?

The same time as the BeCrop Test or sooner is possible: Biome Makers states usually 4 to 6 weeks from the time samples reach its lab. You also save the days a sampling kit takes to arrive, because you take and send the sample right after ordering. We confirm the expected date for your order.

Are VAT, shipping and sampling materials included?

Prices include VAT where it applies; shipping is extra. Companies can enter their EU VAT number at checkout. After your order we send sampling instructions for your location and arrange delivery of the samples to the laboratory; the shipping cost depends on your country and is agreed with you before the samples are sent. If you want everything confirmed in writing before you pay, write to us first.

Does the EU Soil Monitoring Law require farmers to run this test?

No. Directive (EU) 2025/2360 lists DNA metabarcoding of fungi and bacteria as the soil biodiversity descriptor, but it obliges Member States to monitor soils, not farmers to buy tests. BioFiling is a tool for your own crop decisions, not a compliance certificate.
