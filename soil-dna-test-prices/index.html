Not in the table, and why

  • Rhizologic, Portugal (Lost in Cloud, Lda) lists soil metabarcoding (16S / ITS) at €150 per sample, €140 above 10 samples. It cannot be ordered or paid for online: its pages offer a request for analysis, with no basket or payment. Its page describes a diversity profile of bacteria and/or fungi.
  • Agro Smart Lab, Poland sells PCR panels for chosen pathogens of one crop. That is a different method from sequencing the whole soil community.
  • Many other laboratories in the EU sequence soil DNA but publish no price: they sell by quote.
